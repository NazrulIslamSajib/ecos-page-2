    Thoughtfully formulated

    Made with plant-powered active ingredients, our concentrated laundry detergent helps remove everyday stains while protecting colors and caring for fabrics. Vegan, thoughtfully formulated, and gentle enough for sensitive skin, it's a simple way to get a powerful clean with every load.

    Full ingredients list

    Water, Cocamidopropyl Betaine (plant-derived surfactant), Sodium Coco-Sulfate (plant-derived surfactant), Cocamidopropylamine Oxide (plant-derived surfactant), Phenoxyethanol (preservative), Methylisothiazolinone (preservative)

    How to use

    For HE machines, use 1 ounce per load. For standard machines, use 2 ounces. Works well in all water temperatures. For tough stains, apply a small amount directly to the fabric before washing. Always follow garment care instructions.
